Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Hard money lending is simpler than it sounds. Here are the questions we hear most.

How fast can you actually fund?

We typically move from approval to funding in 7–10 days. Traditional banks average 25–40+ days. If speed is part of your deal strategy, that difference is significant.

What do I need to qualify?

Decent credit, real cash in the deal, and a solid opportunity. We look at the whole picture — who you are, what the deal looks like, and whether the numbers make sense.

Do you work with first-time investors?

Yes. We work with investors on their first or second flip. What matters is the deal quality, your credit, and your cash position — not just your track record length.

What types of properties do you fund?

We specialize in fix-and-flip projects and short-term investment property financing. If you have a deal, let's talk through it — the conversation is free.

Is Good Life Lending a lender of last resort?

No. We're selective because we want our borrowers to succeed. We fund the right people with the right deals. If we can't get there on a deal, we'll tell you why.

How is this different from a bank loan?

Speed, flexibility, and who makes the decision. At GLL, you're talking directly to Josh — not waiting on a committee. And our underwriting weighs character and deal quality, not just credit boxes.

Do I have to come to an office?

No. Good Life Lending operates 100% digitally. Everything happens online and over the phone — no commute required.

What happens if my deal doesn't fit?

We'll tell you straight. If a deal doesn't work for us, we'll say so and explain why. No stringing you along. We'd rather you find the right capital than waste your time with the wrong one.
